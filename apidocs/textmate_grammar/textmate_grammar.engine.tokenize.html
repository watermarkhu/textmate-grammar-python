textmate_grammar.engine.tokenize¶

The vscode-textmate v9.3.2 state model and line tokenization engine.

The main loop follows grammar/tokenizeString.ts. Scope names and state are kept as immutable parent-linked structures, while enter/anchor positions are line-local mutable bookkeeping exactly as in StateStackImpl.
