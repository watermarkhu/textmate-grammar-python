Python grammar
--------------
The grammar is vendored from the Microsoft VS Code Python extension at
https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/blob/main/extensions/python/syntaxes/MagicPython.tmLanguage.json
It identifies its original source as MagicPython, maintained at
https://github.com/MagicStack/MagicPython. The grammar is distributed under the
MIT license; the upstream license is available at
https://github.com/MagicStack/MagicPython/blob/master/LICENSE.
